## Context

SportApp is a court-booking platform (padel, football and more) with three parts: backend, a web panel for venues and a mobile app for customers. My main work is the **backend**.

## Decisions

- **Hexagonal, by module:** 16 modules: auth, client-auth, authz, users, bookings, catalog, venues, scheduling, payments, promotions, reviews, search, media, audit, currency and admin. `createApp()` wires the adapters and `BookingRepository` is the domain’s central port.
- **Errors as values:** a `Result` type (`ok`/`err`) and a `DomainError` hierarchy. Use cases don’t throw on business rules; they return a result that the HTTP adapter translates.
- **Two identities:** the customer booking from the app and the venue employee with roles and permissions share neither a model nor an auth flow.
- **Validation at the edge** with Zod 4, and passwords hashed with argon2.
- **Rules that live in the database:** a GiST constraint (above) rules out double bookings, each booking freezes its USD price, the applied exchange rate and the bolívar price so a later rate change can’t alter it, each venue’s data is isolated with RLS, and the audit log is append-only, with UPDATE and DELETE revoked.

## Testing

89 test cases in 21 Vitest files, including **64 fast-check properties** across 13 files. Instead of a few fixed examples, they generate many inputs and check that the domain keeps its invariants.

## Being honest about authorship

- **Backend:** mine.
- **Web panel** (Vite 7, React 19, React Router 7, Tailwind 4, React Query): initial commit by me.
- **Mobile app** (Expo 54 / React Native): started by another developer. My contribution was a large integration commit that brought it to its current state.
